I updated the demo to a simple Alice/Bob inbox.

Alice signs in and sends Bob a note. Bob signs in, reads it and replies.
Alice then signs back in to see the reply. Supabase handles the real login
and database access rules.

I removed the report download, copied-token panel, raw-token display, Google
section and second-account verification controls from the page. We'll inspect
