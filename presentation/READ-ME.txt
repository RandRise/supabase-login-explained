THE IMPORTANT SWAGGER DISTINCTION
The portal and Swagger can hold different tokens.
Use portal session copies whichever account is currently stored by the portal.
Swagger's existing bearer survives a portal account switch until replaced.
The response student_id identifies the owner of the recorded attempt.
Submitting in Swagger does not refresh the portal's displayed score.
To inspect the new score, sign into the relevant account and click View my result.

OPTIONAL REHEARSAL QUESTION
"Bob is signed in on the portal, but Swagger sends Alice's token.
Whose result changes?"
Answer: Alice's, because the server authorizes the token on that request.
